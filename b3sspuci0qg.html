So in conclusion, large droplets settle quickly in the vicinity of the ejection point with little drying, whilst small droplets, less than about 100 µm, quickly dry and remain airborne. This has two major implications for the public health: firstly, safe distance is considerably longer than the generally-recommended two metres, as the virus remains fully suspended in air. Secondly, mask filters available to the public are inefficient in preventing SARS-CoV-2 spreading, as it is most penetrative through fabric filters. They can retain the droplets from nasal and throat secretions on coughing and sneezing, but cannot protect a person from inhaling nearby viruses released into the atmosphere. The deadliness of this virus has drawn deservedly great attention of the scientific community on its spreading mechanism, with the latest re-evaluation of the basis adopted by the World Health Organization and other agencies (9).
Implications
So based on the above considerations and current uncertainties on the spreading process of SARS-CoV-2, we conclude the following implications for public healthcare:
  1. For an uninfected person, wearing face masks may not provide effective protection, as the virus can penetrate the mask like smoke does.
  2. For an infected person who knows that they are infected, it is essential to follow the centuries-old advice and stay inside; going out will infect others, similar to people inhaling cigarette smoke of smokers in the vicinity.  Wearing a high efficiency mask will filter out the droplets emitted from your nose and throat.
  3. For an infected person who does not know that they are infected, again to protect the public, it is essential to wear a high efficiency mask.
  4. As the virus spreads like smoke, safe distance is impractically long.
  5. The acid test for a high efficiency filter is that whilst wearing it, you should not sniff any cigarette smoke if somebody smokes in your vicinity.
  6. The rapid spread of virus is clearly indicative of the ease with which it spreads through the air in a way analogous to spreading of cigarette smoke.
